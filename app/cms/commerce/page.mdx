# Commerce

Commerce features are split across product, currency, transaction, and affiliate
modules.

## Products

Module: `misaf/vendra-product`

Resources:

- Product Categories
- Products
- Product Prices

Products support categories, media, inventory state, stock thresholds, and
prices per currency.

## Currency

Module: `misaf/vendra-currency`

Resources:

- Currency Categories
- Currencies

Currency records support ordering, media, buy/sell price actions, and default
currency behavior.

## Transactions

Module: `misaf/vendra-transaction`

Resources:

- Transactions
- Transaction Gateways

Transaction types include deposit, withdrawal, commission, bonus, and transfer.
Statuses include approved, declined, failed, pending, review, and processing.

## Affiliate

Module: `misaf/vendra-affiliate`

Resources:

- Affiliates
- Affiliate Users

Affiliate resources connect referral and commission workflows to user accounts.

# Content Management

Content Management is backed by the modules that register Filament resources
under the admin panel content group.

## Blog

Module: `misaf/vendra-blog`

Resources:

- Blog Post Categories
- Blog Posts

The blog module is tenant-aware and supports translatable content through the
Filament admin resources.

## Custom Pages

Module: `misaf/vendra-custom-page`

Resources:

- Custom Page Categories
- Custom Pages

Use this module for static pages such as company pages, policy pages, or
landing-page content.

## FAQ

Module: `misaf/vendra-faq`

Resources:

- FAQ Categories
- FAQs

FAQ records are category-based, translatable, sortable, tenant-aware, and can
attach media.

## Multimedia

Module: `misaf/vendra-multimedia`

Resources:

- Multimedia

The multimedia module provides the media library model, Filament resource, and
media-related table/widget support used by content and catalog modules.

## Tagger

Module: `misaf/vendra-tagger`

Resources:

- Taggers

Tagging support is used across entities that need classification or labels.

## Language

Module: `misaf/vendra-language`

Resources:

- Languages
- Language Lines

Language lines are grouped by areas such as authentication, billing, content,
core, dashboard, emails, modules, navigation, notifications, settings, users,
validation, and website.
